THE FRAMEWORK

LineFoundry Methodology

25%

Handicapper quality

Long-term ROI, sample size, NFL-specific and prop-specific performance.

20%

Independent consensus

Agreement among reputable sources, weighted by source quality.

20%

Model edge

Projection versus line, usage, matchup, injuries and game script.

25%

Sportsbook value

Implied probability, estimated probability, EV and line sensitivity.

10%

Market context

Movement and cross-book price/line differences.

Core rule: Most likely to hit ≠ best bet. The recommendation must survive the price.

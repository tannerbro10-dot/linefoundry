HOW WE SCORE SOURCES

Consensus, Not Hype

A single famous analyst is a signal. Multiple independent sources agreeing is stronger.

1

Identify the pick

Player, market, side and the line the analyst actually referenced.

2

3

Find agreement

Group matching player/market/side signals from independent sources.

4

Early, Medium or High based on source quality and independent agreement.

THE FRAMEWORK

LineFoundry Methodology

25%

Handicapper quality

Long-term ROI, sample size, NFL-specific and prop-specific performance.

20%

Independent consensus

Agreement among reputable sources, weighted by source quality.

20%

Model edge

Projection versus line, usage, matchup, injuries and game script.

25%

Sportsbook value

Implied probability, estimated probability, EV and line sensitivity.

10%

Market context

Movement and cross-book price/line differences.

Core rule: Most likely to hit ≠ best bet. The recommendation must survive the price.
